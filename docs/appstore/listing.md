## English (U.S.)

**Name:** Estimate & 3D Measure
**Subtitle:** House scan, cost estimates, AI
**Promotional text:** Walk around a house with your iPhone and get a precise 3D model with wall, window and roof dimensions — plus areas for your estimate in minutes.

**Description:**
Measure a whole house in minutes and turn it into numbers you can price.

3D HOUSE MEASUREMENT
• Walk around the house with your iPhone — a coverage ring shows what is already captured.
• iPhone Pro with LiDAR: dimensions accurate to centimeters (fractions of an inch).
• Get a 3D model, wall lengths, eave and ridge heights, roof pitch and overhangs.
• Windows and doors are found automatically, with width, height and sill height.
• Every value is labeled: measured, from photos, or estimated — no guesswork hidden.
• Hard-to-reach side next to a neighbor or fence? Mark it — the app completes it from the adjacent walls.
• Feet and inches or meters — your choice.

AREAS FOR YOUR ESTIMATE
• Roof area with overhangs, siding area minus windows and doors, gutter length, perimeter and footprint.

ESTIMATES AND TOOLS
• Cost estimates from project documents, material calculators (concrete, tile, masonry, drywall, roofing and more).
• AR tape measure, photo-based counting of rebar and pipes, interior design from a photo.
• AI assistant that explains norms and quantities and reviews your estimate.

Your scans are processed on our server and are only visible to you.

**Keywords:** measure,house,roof,scan,lidar,3d,estimate,contractor,siding,gutter,area,takeoff,pitch,ar,tape

**What to Test (TestFlight):** Try the new 3D house measurement: Home → “3D house measurement”. Walk around a house, then open “My measurements” after 1–3 minutes. Please check dimensions against a tape measure and tell us what looked wrong.

## 简体中文

**名称：** 估价与3D测量
**副标题：** AI 房屋扫描与估价
**推广文本：** 拿着 iPhone 绕房屋走一圈，几分钟内即可得到精确的 3D 模型，包含墙体、门窗和屋顶尺寸，以及估价所需的面积。

**描述：**
几分钟测量整栋房屋，并转换为可直接估价的数据。

房屋 3D 测量
• 拿着 iPhone 绕房屋行走，覆盖环显示已扫描的部分。
• 带 LiDAR 的 iPhone Pro：精度可达厘米级。
• 3D 模型、墙长、檐口与屋脊高度、屋顶坡度和挑檐。
• 自动识别门窗，给出宽度、高度和窗台高度。
• 每个数值都标明来源：实测、照片推算或估计。
• 某一侧紧贴邻居或围墙？标记后，应用会根据相邻墙体自动补全。

估价所需面积
• 含挑檐的屋顶面积、扣除门窗的外墙面积、天沟长度、周长和占地面积。

估价与工具
• 按图纸估价、材料计算器、AR 卷尺、按照片清点钢筋、按照片做室内设计，以及 AI 助手。

您的扫描在我们的服务器上处理，仅您本人可见。

**关键词：** 测量,房屋,屋顶,扫描,激光雷达,3D,估价,施工,外墙,天沟,面积,AR
